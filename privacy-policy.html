Privacy Policy

This application does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Permissions

The CAMERA permission is used exclusively to control the flashlight functionality of the device. This application does not capture photos or record videos.

Some preferences such as vibration settings, brightness options, and auto-off timers are stored locally on the device only.

Internet Connection

This application does not require an internet connection to function.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact
